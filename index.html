      How to play

      The board shows a recipe tree: the hidden finished item on top, its components below. There are three endless modes:

      • BBuild: the full recipe is shown. Name the finished item.
      • IImposter: one extra component doesn't belong. Tap it, then name the finished item.
      • MMissing: one component is hidden behind the ? tile. Name it, then name the finished item.

      You get 6 attempts. Correct answers lock in, and every puzzle has exactly one answer. Every component shows its gold price and stats. After 3 guesses, the hint panel unlocks the finished item's gold and stats.

      Each mode keeps its own stats and streak. Giving up counts as a loss.
