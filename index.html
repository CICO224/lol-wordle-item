Recipe Rift

Puzzle

    How to play

    The board shows the recipe of a finished item, but it has been tampered with. There are two endless modes:

    • IImposter: every real component is shown, plus one that doesn't belong. Tap the imposter.
    • MMissing: one real component is hidden behind the ? tile. Name it.
    • +In both modes, also name the finished item the real recipe builds into.

    You get 6 attempts. Correct answers lock in. Every item shows its gold price, and the Oracle tells you whether the answer costs more or less than your guess.

    Each mode keeps its own stats and streak. Giving up counts as a loss.
