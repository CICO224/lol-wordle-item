Recipe Rift

Puzzle
  1. Spot the imposter component
  2. Name the missing component
  3. Guess the finished item

How to play

The board shows the recipe of a finished item — but it has been tampered with.

  • 1One shown component is an imposter: it is not in the recipe. Tap it.
  • 2One real component is missing (the ? tile). Name it.
  • 3Name the finished item the real recipe builds into.

You get 6 attempts. Each attempt submits all three answers; correct answers lock in. The Oracle tells you whether the right answer costs more or less and which stats you share.

Daily: one puzzle per day, same for everyone. Endless: as many as you like, track your streak.
